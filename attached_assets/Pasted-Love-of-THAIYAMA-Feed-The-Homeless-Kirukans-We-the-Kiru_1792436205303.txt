Love of THAIYAMA || Feed The Homeless || Kirukans
We, the Kirukans team, did a social work “FEED THE HOMELESS” under a bridge in Trichy.Through this event we distributed grocery items required for a month to the needy people who are more likely to experience adverse economic challenges, poorer health, unemployment and higher poverty rates.

The first family we visited was Thaiyamma and Muthu..

Thaiyamma and muthu
Thaiyamma seeks alms at a temple and Muthu does rag picking for their livelihood. Recently they have shifted to a small rented house hut in a crooked locality. As we walked through their doorstep, they greeted us with love and affection as if they do to their own children.

There may have been many tragic events in their lives, but they held on to the hope of life that gave them the strength to move forward each day. When we have nothing that belong to us, and imagine how it would feel to have something that you could actually call mine?. In the same way they called each other mine.

Isn’t love enough to pick us up when we fall?

They met in a temple, fell in love, accepted each other’s flaws, and began life with new hope. They taught us that love isn’t just about admiring the things which we have, it’s about admiring even the thing we don’t have. And it made us realize that even in the modern age of love stories, Muthu and Thaiyamma continue to be the goal of a true couple.

As we sat around her and talked like her children, her eyes lit up and her heart felt immeasurably happy. Their love for each other made us wonder if it wouldn’t be blessed to live a beautiful life as them.
