---
name: Impact motion integrity
description: Keep NGO impact animations honest and accessible rather than simulating live activity.
---

Treat impact animation as presentation, not new impact data. Never add synthetic live increases or infer totals from visitor activity.

**Why:** The user requested animation and interaction for the existing NGO impact section, not changing its claims or introducing live metrics.

**How to apply:** Animate towards the approved figures and preserve their qualifiers. Keep the final values available to assistive technology and show them immediately for reduced-motion users; animation stages are not separate factual totals.
