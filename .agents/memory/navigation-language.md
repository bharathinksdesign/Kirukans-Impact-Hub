name: Navigation language
description: User-approved preference for clear, action-specific menu wording.

Keep navigation wording direct and action-specific; use “Volunteer” for ways to take part, not donation or payment calls to action.

**Why:** The user asked to remove donation functionality and related calls to action from the site.

**How to apply:** Keep volunteering clearly distinct from impact stories and totals. Do not add donation links or payment options unless the user asks.

About us and Volunteer have dedicated pages. Our work lives in the homepage's What we do section, not a separate page or menu entry.

**Why:** The user initially asked for a separate Our work page, then asked to remove it and its menu entry while retaining the homepage section.

**How to apply:** Do not re-add Our work to navigation or footer links; send visitors to the homepage section or Impact stories instead. Keep About us and Our team distinct.
